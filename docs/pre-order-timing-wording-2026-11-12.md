# Pre-order timing wording (12 Nov 2026)

Why: the site told customers the lab only gets a pre-order once the batch closes. In practice
work can start as soon as the 48-hour cancellation window has passed, mixed orders are switched
to ready-to-ship stock when it exists, and orders are moved into earlier shipments when there is
room. Customers were surprised when a pre-order could not be cancelled before the cut-off.

## The three lines

- **Main line:** Once the 48-hour cancellation window has passed, we may start working on your
  pre-order right away, even before the batch cut-off. The cut-off is the last day to join a
  batch, not the day work begins, so your order may arrive earlier than the estimate.
- **Mixed orders:** If you order ready-to-ship and pre-order plants together and we have your
  pre-order variety in ready-to-ship stock, it is automatically switched to the ready-to-ship
  plant so your order ships sooner. That plant is already rooted and acclimated, not a sealed
  plantlet. If you would rather wait for the plantlet, reply to your order email and we will
  keep it as a pre-order.
- **Earlier batch:** When there's room, we move orders into an earlier shipment so they arrive
  sooner.
