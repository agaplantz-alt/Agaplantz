# AgaPlantz scout log

A daily research run adds new product and business ideas here, newest first. These are leads to check, not decisions.
Anything marked (unverified) could not be confirmed from a public source. Prices are from public listings on the day
of the run, usually in USD, and change often.

Already on the store as of 2026-11-02, so these are not new ideas: Monstera Devil Monster, Bulbasaur, White Monster
and Creme Brulee; Alocasia Cuprea Red Secret and Bambino Pink; Anthurium warocqueanum; the monthly box, the mystery box,
the winter heat-pack add-on and the 30-day guarantee.

### Product ideas

**1. Cheap TC of classic velvet Anthurium species: forgetii, luxurians and clarinervium.**
- What: three well-known species Anthuriums as TC plantlets. The crystallinum x regale idea on 2026-11-05 covered
  hybrids; these are the parent species collectors start with.
- Why now: one US lab lists forgetii TC at $6.99 USD, luxurians at $12.99 and clarinervium at $16.99, while another
  shop sells forgetii and clarinervium TC at $39.99, forgetii sold out. At the very top, a variegated luxurians sold
  for $26,000 on Palmstreet, an outlier from older reporting.
  ([Orange Lake forgetii](https://orangelakenursery.com/products/anthurium-forgetti-plantlet-1),
  [Orange Lake luxurians](https://orangelakenursery.com/products/anthurium-luxurians-tissue-culture),
  [TropicFlow clarinervium](https://m.reach.dog/shop/tropicflow/products/anthurium-clarinervium-tissue-culture-plant),
  [Forbes Australia](https://www.forbes.com.au/?p=164991))
- Price signal: $7 to $17 USD per plantlet at one lab against $40 at another; a 4-inch forgetii x luxurians at
  $59.99. ([BWH Plant Co](https://bwhplantco.com/collections/rare-plants/products/4-anthurium-forgetii-x-luxurians))

## 2026-11-11

Evidence was thin again: retail listings and blogs, plus one CTV News story. A theme today is plants losing their
rarity as tissue culture spreads.

### Product ideas

**1. Variegated Monstera 'Peru' (Monstera karstenianum).**
- What: a small, stiff-leaved climbing Monstera, in a variegated form. The store's Monsteras are all deliciosa types.
- Why now: US shops list variegated 'Peru' at about $40 to $90 USD, and one tracker shows a rise from $55 to $60.95.
  European wholesale lists it at about €28.79.
  ([Plant Vault](https://reach.dog/shop/plant-vault/products/variegated-monstera-peru),
  [Floraccess](https://www.floraccess.com/en/product/99061/monstera-peru-variegata),
  [Secret Garden](https://secretgarden.ro/en/products/monstera-peru-variegata-monstera-karstenianum))
- Price signal: about €29 wholesale against $40 to $90 USD retail. No TC price found (unverified).
- Difficulty: medium. Sellers mix up the names 'Peru', obliqua and adansonii, so confirm the species with your
  supplier.
- First step: ask your lab or a European wholesaler for variegated karstenianum and a price per 50.

**2. Variegated Monstera adansonii, including tricolour.**
- What: the "Swiss cheese vine" with white or tricolour variegation, a cheaper way into variegated Monstera.
- Why now: standard variegated adansonii sells for about $18 to $25 USD, and one rooted tricolour cutting lists at
  about $118, so the tricolour form carries a big premium.
  ([Floraccess](https://www.floraccess.com/en/product/78760/monstera-adansonii-variegata/),
  [tricolour listing](https://ava.netmd.org/products/rare-tricolor-variegation-rooted-adansonii-plant-monstera/53224496/))
  Collector demand for tricolour was not confirmed (unverified).
- Price signal: €20.72 wholesale for the standard form at Floraccess.
- Difficulty: easy to medium. It grows fast and roots easily.
- First step: price the standard variegated form from a wholesaler and test it beside your Monstera range.

**3. Philodendron 'Pink Princess' only as a cheap volume plant.**
- What: the once-famous pink-variegated Philodendron, priced as an entry plant rather than a collector piece.
- Why now: tissue culture has made it common. A 2025 article says it "isn't rare anymore", a blog puts the median US
  TC price around $45, and Walmart lists a 5-inch plant at about $23 USD.
  ([Soltech](https://soltech.com/blogs/blog/pink-princess-philodendron-rare-in-2025),
  [The Plant Network](https://theplantnetwork.pages.dev/blog/rare-vs-trendy-plants),
  [Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/ip/2281343152))
- Price signal: about $23 to $45 USD now. Only worth stocking if your cost is well under that (unverified).
- Difficulty: easy.
- First step: compare your lab's Pink Princess cost against $23 retail before deciding.

  trend list names Silver Dragon, Pink Dragon and Frydek among sought-after varieties, though that list is weak
  evidence.
  ([Palmstreet 2025 takeaways](https://palmstreet.app/blog/palmstreets-2025-takeaways),
  [Accio Alocasia trends](https://de.accio.com/business/alocasia-trends),
  [Soltech Alocasia guide](https://soltech.com/blogs/blog/most-expensive-alocasia-plants-rare-coveted-varieties))
- Price signal: no TC prices found for these today (unverified).

- First step: ask your lab which jewel Alocasias are on its next batch list.

**2. Variegated Schismatoglottis and Homalomena.**
- What: two small, shade-tolerant aroid genera that suit terrariums and shelves. The store doesn't carry either.
- Why now: several shops sell variegated forms, and some list them sold out. One offers a variegated Homalomena
  rubescens as a TC plantlet.
  ([Foliage Factory](https://www.foliage-factory.com/schismatoglottis-longispatha-variegata),
  [Rare Plant Fairy](https://www.rareplantfairy.com/collections/growers-choice-1/products/schismatoglottis-sp-albo-growers-choice),

  [OrchidBox Homalomena TC](https://m.reach.dog/shop/orchidbox/products/homalomena-rubescens-variegated-preorder-1813p-3-us-based-seller-rare-aroid))
- Price signal: Schismatoglottis variegata at €30.75 on sale; a variegated albo form at $55 USD on sale from $95;
  Homalomena TC at $77.98 USD. Demand was not confirmed (unverified).
- Difficulty: medium. Few labs carry them, so supply is the hurdle.
- First step: ask your lab whether it has any variegated Schismatoglottis or Homalomena.

**3. Rooted cuttings for plants that don't clone well in a lab.**
- What: sell rooted node cuttings of variegated plants such as Monstera 'Albo', alongside your TC range.
- Why now: a lab-services company writes that "periclinal chimeras" such as Monstera 'Albo' don't multiply well in
  tissue culture while keeping their variegation, so supply still depends on cuttings. Meanwhile, plants that do
  clone well have dropped to under $50 at many retailers. That company sells lab services, so treat its claims with
  care (unverified).
  ([Plant Cell Technology](https://plantcelltechnology.com/blogs/blog/how-plant-tissue-culture-changed-the-rare-plant-market-a-scientific-breakdown),
  [Orange Lake blog](https://orangelakenursery.com/blogs/orange-lake-nursery-blog/what-is-tissue-culture-and-how-it-shapes-the-plant-market))
- Price signal: none found for Albo cuttings today (unverified).
- Difficulty: medium. It needs a mother plant and rooting space, but no lab.
- First step: check whether any mature specimen you already own can be cut for nodes.

Evidence was thin again: retail listings, third-party price trackers and app-vendor blogs, no hard sales data.

### Product ideas

**1. Philodendron melanochrysum, the "black gold" climber.**
- What: the parent species, sold on its own. The 'Glorious' hybrid logged on 2026-11-04 is a cross of this plant
  and gloriosum; this is the pure species, which many collectors want too.
- Why now: third-party price trackers show small US pots rising. One went from $25 to $30 USD, another from $27 to
  $32 and then $35. European wholesale still lists small pots at about €7 to €9.
  ([Spacecraft Vapor 4-inch](https://reach.dog/shop/spacecraft-vapor/products/04-philodendron-melanochrysum),
  [Thai Exotic Greenery](https://reach.dog/shop/thai-exotic-greenery/products/philodendron-melanochrysum),

- Price signal: about €7 wholesale against $30 to $35 USD retail. The price rises come from tracker sites and may not
  be market-wide (unverified).
- Difficulty: medium. It climbs, so it sells best with a moss pole (see idea 3).
- First step: ask your lab or a Canadian wholesaler for melanochrysum plugs and price per 50.

**2. Philodendron gloriosum, the crawling velvet Philodendron.**
- What: a velvet-leaf Philodendron that creeps along the soil instead of climbing, so it needs a long, shallow pot.
- Why now: one US shop calls it sought after by collectors, and a tracker shows its price rising from $19.99 to
  $24.99 USD before a later listing at $35, marked down from $55.

**3. Extendable moss poles, bought wholesale.**
