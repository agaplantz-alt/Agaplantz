# AgaPlantz scout log

A daily research run adds new product and business ideas here, newest first. These are leads to check, not decisions.
Anything marked (unverified) could not be confirmed from a public source. Prices are from public listings on the day
of the run, usually in USD, and change often.

Already on the store as of 2026-11-02, so these are not new ideas: Monstera Devil Monster, Bulbasaur, White Monster
and Creme Brulee; Alocasia Cuprea Red Secret and Bambino Pink; Anthurium warocqueanum; the monthly box, the mystery box,
the winter heat-pack add-on and the 30-day guarantee.

## 2026-11-02 (scheduled run, 08:40 Toronto)

Second run today. It covers angles the earlier run did not. Evidence was thin: almost every source is a seller listing,
so treat the prices as snapshots, not trends.

### Product ideas

**1. Variegated Alocasia plantlets.**
- What: variegated Alocasia such as 'Heart Balloon Variegated', 'Ninja Aurea' and 'Jacklyn Mystic Variegated'. The
  store's Alocasias today are Cuprea Red Secret and Bambino Pink.
- Why now: variegated Alocasia is being actively bred and patented. A new marbled cultivar, 'ELSTCIRCU' from a Dutch
  program, had its US plant patent published on 2026-08-04, so more variegated lines are coming.
  ([patent record](https://eureka.patsnap.com/patent/USPP37565P3))
- Price signal: one US lab lists TC plantlets at $79.99 USD for Heart Balloon Variegated and $29.99 for Ninja Aurea.
  ([Heart Balloon](https://orangelakenursery.com/products/alocasia-heart-ballon-varigated-tissue-culture),
  [Ninja Aurea](https://orangelakenursery.com/products/alocasia-ninja-aurea-tissue-culture))
  Collector demand for these specific cultivars was not confirmed (unverified).
- Difficulty: medium. Same lab and acclimation path as your current Alocasias.
- First step: ask your lab which variegated Alocasias it has in its next batch and the price per 100.

**2. A low-cost "first rare plant" tier.**
- What: common but attractive Alocasia plantlets sold cheaply as an entry point and a stocking stuffer, alongside the
  collector plants.
- Why now: Canadian holiday budgets are shrinking (see the corporate gifts idea above), and some Alocasia TC is very
  cheap: $7.99 USD for Venusta and $5.99 for 'Loco' at one US lab.
  ([Venusta](https://orangelakenursery.com/products/alocasia-venusta-tissue-culture-single),
  [Loco](https://orangelakenursery.com/products/alocasia-loco-tissue-culture-single))
- Price signal: the wide gap between cheap TC and grown-plant prices suggests room for margin, but your Canadian cost
  after shipping and phyto was not checked (unverified).
- Difficulty: easy, if your lab already grows them.
- First step: pick two cheap, hardy species and test them as a $20 to $30 CAD ready-to-ship option.

**3. Rare species Begonias.**
- What: collector Begonias such as B. amphioxus and B. melanobullata. The store lists Begonia as a genus, but none
  showed on the homepage this morning.
- Why now: sellers describe these as small-batch "collector releases", and GrowTropicals says tissue culture is now
  making rare species available at scale.
  ([GrowTropicals](https://growtropicals.com/collections/rare-and-unusual?page=34),
  [Cultivo Carnivores](https://www.cultivocarnivores.com/products/begonia-amphioxus))
- Price signal: B. amphioxus shows at $9.99 USD in a 2.5-inch pot on a Walmart listing; B. melanobullata at $45.60.
  These are single listings (unverified as a trend).
  ([Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/c/kp/begonia-maculum),
  [melanobullata listing](https://blog.onparticipe.fr/products/rare-begonia-melanobullata-exact-plant/15434386))
- Difficulty: medium. Many species Begonias need high humidity, which pairs with the cabinet kit idea above.
- First step: check whether your lab offers species Begonias at all.

### Business ideas

**1. Wholesale tissue-culture plantlets to Canadian plant shops.**
- What: sell acclimated plantlets in bulk to independent plant shops across Canada.
- Why now: no Canadian wholesale supplier of rare aroid TC turned up. The suppliers that did, such as Rare Root Labs
  and Silver Krome Gardens, are in the US, which means shops face border paperwork you already handle.
  ([Rare Root Labs reviews](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/rarerootlabs.com),
  [Greenhouse Canada buyers guide](https://www.greenhousecanada.com/company/agriforest-bio-technologies-ltd-2222/))
  The supply gap itself is unverified: the search may simply have missed Canadian sellers.
- Price signal: one US wholesale listing on Faire shows $119 per Caramel Marble plantlet against a $150 MSRP
  (from the earlier run). No Canadian wholesale prices found (unverified).
- Difficulty: medium. It needs a wholesale price list and minimum order.
- First step: call or message three GTA plant shops and ask where they buy rare aroids today and what they pay.

**2. Amazon.ca, check before acting.**
- What: list accessories, and maybe plants, on Amazon.ca.
- Why now: Amazon restricts live plants to approved categories and removes listings that break the rules, and its US
  marketplace has removed plants sold by non-US sellers. No Amazon.ca-specific rules were found.
  ([Amazon restricted-category summary](https://www.goatconsulting.com/amazon-policy/amazon-plant-and-seed-products-restricted-category-policy),
  [Better Seed](https://www.betterseed.org/amazon-to-remove-listings-for-seeds-for-planting-by-non-u-s-residents/))
- Price signal: none (unverified).
- Difficulty: easy for accessories such as the cabinet kit, hard for live plants.
- First step: start with non-living accessories only. Check Amazon.ca Seller Central's plant policy before listing
  any plant.

## 2026-11-02

**Market backdrop.** Seller blogs agree that collectors are moving away from mass-produced "rare" plants toward
unusual forms and specific variegation. Tissue culture is also pushing prices down on cultivars that used to be
premium, such as Thai Constellation and Pink Princess. Neither claim comes with hard sales data.
([Soltech](https://soltech.com/blogs/blog/the-ultimate-status-symbols-most-expensive-houseplants-in-2026),
[Gardener's Yards](https://gardenersyards.com/houseplant-trends-2026/),
[GrowTropicals](https://growtropicals.com/blogs/growingtropicals/trending-houseplants-for-2026))

### Product ideas

**1. Velvet Anthurium hybrids ('King of Spades' and 'Ace of Spades' crosses).**
- What: dark, velvet-leaf Anthurium hybrids sold as tissue-culture plantlets. The store's only Anthurium is warocqueanum.
- Why now: Soltech names velvet-leaf hybrids like 'King of Spades' as rising among collectors.
  ([Soltech](https://soltech.com/blogs/blog/the-ultimate-status-symbols-most-expensive-houseplants-in-2026))
- Price signal: Orange Lake Nursery lists an 'Ace of Spades' TC plantlet at $15.99 USD. A 'King of Spades' seedling
  sells for $124.99 USD and a finished plant for £69.99, so the spread from plantlet to grown plant is wide.
  ([Orange Lake, Ace of Spades TC](https://orangelakenursery.com/products/anthurium-ace-of-spades-plantlet-1),
  [Steve's Leaves](https://stevesleaves.com/products/anthurium-king-of-spades-x-doc-block-f2),
  [Cowells](https://www.cowellsgc.co.uk/product/anthurium-king-of-spades))
  The 'King of Spades' TC price did not show on the page (unverified).
  ([Orange Lake, King of Spades TC](https://orangelakenursery.com/products/anthurium-king-of-spades-hybrid-tissue-culture.oembed))
- Difficulty: medium. It needs a lab source that can ship to Canada with a phytosanitary certificate, plus a few
  months of acclimation before ready-to-ship.
- First step: ask your current TC lab whether it has 'Ace of Spades' or 'King of Spades' crosses, and at what
  price per 100.

**2. Philodendron 'Caramel Marble' tissue culture.**
- What: a variegated Philodendron with caramel, cream and green marbling.
- Why now: it appears on several 2026 "must-have" lists and is widely stocked as TC by US sellers.
  ([Gardener's Yards](https://gardenersyards.com/rare-variegated-aroids-2026/))
- Price signal: US retail TC listings run from $17.99 to $143 USD. One wholesale listing on Faire shows $119 per
  plantlet against a $150 MSRP. The very wide range means some sellers are discounting hard.
  ([Orange Lake](https://orangelakenursery.com/products/philodendron-caramel-marble-tissue-culture),
  [Dandi Plants on Reach](https://reach.dog/shop/dandi-plants/products/tissue-culture-philodendron-caramel-marble-variegated-sellers-choice),
  [Faire](https://www.faire.com/product/p_nkj6ze7ryt))
- Difficulty: easy to medium, if your current lab already offers it.
- First step: check whether it is on your lab's next batch list. Price it below the US retail middle once
  converted to CAD with shipping. Canadian competitor prices were not checked today (unverified).

**3. "Winter humidity" cabinet kit.**
- What: a bundle of what collectors put inside a glass cabinet, such as an IKEA Milsbo. That means a grow light, a
  thermometer and hygrometer, a moss pole and aroid mix. Sell the inside, not the cabinet.
- Why now: Canadian homes drop to about 35% humidity in winter. One grower reports a stalled plant regrew at 60%
  inside a cabinet, and r/Aroids has an active thread of Milsbo builds running 70% to 90% humidity.
  ([House Plant Journal](https://www.houseplantjournal.com/?p=1398), [r/Aroids](https://lr.in.psf.lt/r/Aroids/top))
- Price signal: none found for a ready-made kit (unverified). Retailers such as Highland Moss sell aroid-mix and
  moss-pole bundles, which shows the pieces sell on their own.
  ([Highland Moss](https://highlandmoss.com/business-and-bulk-sales), [Plantscraper](https://plantscraper.com/collections/substrates))
- Difficulty: easy. No live plants are involved, so there is no shipping-death risk in winter.
- First step: price the parts from a Canadian wholesaler and see whether a kit lands under $100 CAD.

### Business ideas

**1. Corporate holiday plant gifts for December.**
- What: a small, gift-boxed plant with a printed care card, sold in bulk to local companies for clients and staff.
- Why now: Deloitte projects Canadian holiday spending will fall 14% in 2026, so buyers are looking for smaller,
  more personal gifts. A US survey found holiday business gifts should land in the first half of December, which
  leaves about four weeks. Foli already sells plants as Canadian corporate gifts, so the market exists.
  ([Retail Insider on Deloitte](https://retail-insider.com/?p=209496), [Custom Ink](https://www.customink.com/blog/?p=28682),
  [CHCH on Foli](https://www.chch.com/morning-live/foli-spreads-plant-joy-all-across-canada))
- Price or margin signal: none found for Canada (unverified).
- Difficulty: easy to medium. Use hardy, cheaper plants or ready-to-ship TC stock, and deliver locally in the GTA to
  avoid cold-weather shipping.
- First step: make one "Corporate gifts" page with a 10-plant minimum and a contact form. Email five local businesses
  you already know this week.

**2. A GTA winter plant swap or "tissue culture 101" workshop.**
- What: a ticketed in-person event to unbox and pot tissue-culture plantlets, with plants for sale.
- Why now: Toronto plant swaps exist (Toronto Botanical Garden, JOMO Studio), but no rare-plant swap or workshop was
  found for November or December 2026. That gap is unverified.
  ([Toronto Botanical Garden](https://torontobotanicalgarden.ca/events/2026-02-21/?ical=1),
  [JOMO Studio](https://jomostudio.com/products/toronto-plant-swap), [blogTO](https://www.blogto.com/events/toronto-plant-swap-toronto/))
- Price signal: none found (unverified).
- Difficulty: medium. It needs a venue and a date, but no new stock.
- First step: ask JOMO Studio or a local café about hosting one in early December.
