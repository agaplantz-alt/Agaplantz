# AgaPlantz scout log

A daily research run adds new product and business ideas here, newest first. These are leads to check, not decisions.
Anything marked (unverified) could not be confirmed from a public source. Prices are from public listings on the day
of the run, usually in USD, and change often.

Already on the store as of 2026-11-02, so these are not new ideas: Monstera Devil Monster, Bulbasaur, White Monster
and Creme Brulee; Alocasia Cuprea Red Secret and Bambino Pink; Anthurium warocqueanum; the monthly box, the mystery box,
the winter heat-pack add-on and the 30-day guarantee.

## 2026-11-05

Evidence was thin again: seller listings, fee guides and forum posts, no hard sales data.

### Product ideas

**1. Low-cost crystallinum x regale Anthuriums.**
- What: silver-veined Anthurium crosses sold cheaply as TC. This differs from the velvet 'King of Spades' crosses
  logged on 2026-11-02; these are crystallinum and regale, the classic white-veined look.
- Why now: one US lab lists a crystallinum x regale TC at $9.99 USD and regale alone at $12.99, the cheapest
  Anthurium TC seen in this log. Trend demand was not confirmed (unverified).
  ([Regale x crystallinum](https://orangelakenursery.com/products/anthurium-regale-x-crystallium-tissue-culture),
  [Regale](https://orangelakenursery.com/products/anthurium-regale-tissue-culture))
- Price signal: grown crystallinum plants list at $25 USD at Ecuagenera and $42 at Carnivero, so the plantlet-to-plant
  spread is wide. ([Ecuagenera](https://ecuagenera.com/collections/anthurium?page=11),
  [Carnivero](https://www.carnivero.com/products/anthurium-crystallinum))
- Difficulty: easy to medium. Crystallinum types are among the easier Anthuriums to grow on.
- First step: ask your lab for crystallinum x regale on the next batch and price per 100.

**2. Red crystallinum crosses at the premium end.**
- What: newer Anthurium crosses with red-flushed new leaves, such as 'SKG Silver x Red Crystallinum',
  'Tezula Red Crystallinum' and 'Michelle Red Crystallinum'.
- Why now: one US lab carries several of these as TC, which suggests labs are producing them in volume.
  ([SKG Silver x Red](https://orangelakenursery.com/products/anthurium-skg-silver-x-red-crystallinum-tissue-culture),
  [Tezula Red](https://orangelakenursery.com/collections/anthurium/products/anthurium-tezula-red-crystallinum-tissue-culture))
- Price signal: SKG Silver x Red TC at $19.99 USD. Other prices did not show (unverified).
- Difficulty: medium.
- First step: price one red cross from your lab and list it next to the cheaper crystallinum x regale as a
  "good, better" pair.

**3. A house-brand aroid substrate.**
- What: AgaPlantz's own bagged aroid mix, the one you already pot plantlets in, sold in small bags.
- Why now: hobbyist forums report sphagnum moss as hard to find and rising in price, though these reports are
  undated and anecdotal (unverified). Retail sphagnum still sells in volume at about $5 to $8 USD per unit, so
  there is a market for the inputs.
  ([FlytrapCare forum](https://www.flytrapcare.com/phpBB3/post386003.html),
  [ASInsight](https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/spagnum-moss))
- Price signal: none for a branded mix (unverified).
- Difficulty: easy. Not alive, so it ships in winter.
- First step: price your current mix per litre and decide whether a 2-litre bag clears a margin at checkout.

### Business ideas

**1. A drop-off plant clinic.**
- What: customers bring a sick plant to you for pest treatment, repotting or rescue, paying per treatment. This
  differs from the plant-sitting idea logged on 2026-11-03, which visits the customer's home.
- Why now: US plant shops run these with clear menus. One charges $5 to $25 for a first pest treatment and $60 an hour
  for longer care; another started a "plant hospital" in February 2026.
  ([Urban Sprouts pricing](https://urbansproutsstore.com/pages/clinic-pricing-guide),
  [Urban Sprouts process](https://urbansproutsstore.com/pages/clinic-process),
  [9&10 News](https://www.9and10news.com/2026/02/11/northern-michigan-woman-starts-unique-plant-hospital))
- Price signal: US$5 to $60 per treatment or hour from those shops. No revenue figures found (unverified).
- Difficulty: medium. Sick plants bring pests, so they must be kept far from your stock, and the clinic shops
  require a first treatment before admission for the same reason.
- First step: decide whether you have a separate room. If not, skip this idea.

**2. An Etsy shop as a second channel.**
- What: list accessories and a few plants on Etsy to reach buyers who don't know agaplantz.com.
- Why now: Canadian sellers can list in CAD to avoid the 2.5% conversion fee, and sellers under $10,000 can opt out
  of Offsite Ads. Fees are roughly 6.5% per sale plus 3% + CAD $0.25 processing, plus a regulatory fee that guides
  give as either 0.50% or 1.15% for Canada (unverified).
  ([Marmalead](https://blog.marmalead.com/etsy-fees-explained/),
  [Insight Agent Canada guide](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/sell-on-etsy-canada),
  [Crosslist](https://crosslist.com/blog/etsy-review))
- Price signal: about 10% of each sale in fees, by these guides.
- Difficulty: easy for accessories. For live plants, check whether Etsy allows them from Canadian sellers and the
  Canadian Food Inspection Agency rules first.
- First step: list one accessory, such as a substrate bag or heat pack, and watch views for two weeks.

## 2026-11-04

Evidence was thin again: mostly seller listings and SEO blogs. None of today's ideas repeats an earlier one, except
idea 3, which says what changed.

### Product ideas

**1. Velvet Philodendron hybrids: 'Majestic' and 'Glorious'.**
- What: two crosses collectors know by name. 'Majestic' is sodiroi x verrucosum and 'Glorious' is gloriosum x
  melanochrysum. They differ from Caramel Marble, logged on 2026-11-02, which is about variegation; these are about
  velvet leaves.
- Why now: one seller's 2026 guide says demand for specimen-grade Philodendron has held steady.
  ([Soltech](https://soltech.com/en-ca/blogs/blog/the-ultimate-status-symbols-most-expensive-houseplants-in-2026))
- Price signal: both listed at $49.99 on one shop; stock status not shown (unverified).
  ([Carnivero](https://www.carnivero.com/collections/philodendron?page=1))
- Difficulty: medium. They need a lab source that can ship to Canada.
- First step: ask your lab whether it has either hybrid as TC and at what price per 100.

**2. Philodendron 'Billietiae x Jose Buono' and variegated 'Red Congo'.**
- What: two TC Philodendrons at the higher end of the range. One is a cross of two collector species; the other is
  a variegated form of a common, hardy plant.
- Why now: both are being sold as TC by US labs, and Red Congo variegated is offered wholesale on Faire with a
  minimum order of 5.
  ([Billietiae x Jose Buono](https://reach.dog/shop/orange-lake-nursery/products/philodendron-billietiae-x-jose-buono-tissue-culture),
  [Red Congo variegated TC](https://orangelakenursery.com/products/philodendron-variegated-red-congo-tissue-culture),
  [Faire](https://www.faire.com/product/p_8t5br9pprd))
- Price signal: Billietiae x Jose Buono TC at $89.99 USD, marked down from $99.99. Red Congo wholesale price was not
  captured (unverified).
- Difficulty: medium.
- First step: request the Red Congo wholesale price on Faire. A minimum of 5 is a cheap test.

**3. A shelf grow light as a standalone add-on.**
- What: one slim full-spectrum LED bar, sold alongside plants at checkout. The cabinet kit on 2026-11-02 included a
  light; this is the light on its own, for buyers without a cabinet.
- What changed: a Canadian price range and a winter angle turned up today. An Ontario news piece pairs slim LED panels
  with houseplants to offset short winter days and puts a budget panel at about $80 to $120 CAD. Lee Valley sells
  full-spectrum lights from $59.50 CAD.
  ([Georgian Bay News](https://georgianbaynews.com/?p=11476), [Lee Valley](https://www.leevalley.com/en-ca/shop/garden/planting/grow-lights),
  [Pepiniere](https://www.pepiniere.ca/en/collections/lighting-for-plants))
- Price signal: retail $59.50 to $120 CAD. Your wholesale cost was not checked (unverified).
- Difficulty: easy. It is not alive, but buy certified units: one plant blog warns that uncertified grow lights are
  common on online marketplaces.
  ([Mossify](https://www.mossify.ca/blogs/meet-the-bloggers/creating-the-perfect-lighting-environment-for-houseplants-with-a-full-spectrum-led-grow-light-tips-and-best-practices))
- First step: find a Canadian distributor for one certified LED bar and add it as a checkout upsell.

### Business ideas

**1. "Statement plant" add-on for GTA office-plant companies.**
- What: supply rare, eye-catching plants to the companies that already maintain office plants, instead of competing
  with them.
- Why now: the GTA already has established office-plant firms such as Moore Park Plantscapes and Toronto Office
  Plants. One data aggregator estimates Toronto Office Plants at about 20 staff and $4.1 million in revenue
  (unverified). That makes them buyers, not a market to enter cold.
  ([Moore Park on Datanyze](https://www.datanyze.com/companies/moore-park-plantscapes/346870319),
  [Plantation on Datanyze](https://www.datanyze.com/companies/plantation/532552781))
- Price signal: no leasing or wholesale rates found (unverified).
- Difficulty: medium. Offices need tough plants, so start with hardy Philodendron and Monstera, not delicate TC.
- First step: email Moore Park Plantscapes a short note offering a sample of three statement plants.

**2. A behind-the-scenes YouTube or Shorts channel.**
- What: short videos of the lab-to-door process: unboxing TC flasks, acclimation, winter packing. Earn from
  affiliate links and drive store sales.
- Why now: a backyard nursery owner credits his channel's success to filming himself working in his nursery, not
  SEO. Guides say ads are only part of creator income, with affiliate links and sponsorships the rest. Earnings
  figures in these guides are unverified.
  ([Side Hustle Nation](https://www.sidehustlenation.com/the-backyard-nursery-side-hustle/),
  [Fluxnote guide](https://fluxnote.io/guides/start-gardening-youtube-channel-2026))
- Price signal: none reliable (unverified).
- Difficulty: easy to start, slow to pay off.
- First step: film one 30-second clip of the next TC batch being unpacked and post it as a Short and a Reel.

  [Faire](https://www.faire.com/product/p_ndwcsbcf26))
- Difficulty: easy. No live plants needed, so it ships safely in winter.
- First step: get a Canadian wholesale quote for LECA and nutrient solution and price a kit.

**3. Monstera 'Mint' tissue culture.**

## 2026-11-02

**Market backdrop.** Seller blogs agree that collectors are moving away from mass-produced "rare" plants toward
unusual forms and specific variegation. Tissue culture is also pushing prices down on cultivars that used to be
premium, such as Thai Constellation and Pink Princess. Neither claim comes with hard sales data.
([Soltech](https://soltech.com/blogs/blog/the-ultimate-status-symbols-most-expensive-houseplants-in-2026),
[Gardener's Yards](https://gardenersyards.com/houseplant-trends-2026/),
[GrowTropicals](https://growtropicals.com/blogs/growingtropicals/trending-houseplants-for-2026))

### Product ideas

**1. Velvet Anthurium hybrids ('King of Spades' and 'Ace of Spades' crosses).**
- What: dark, velvet-leaf Anthurium hybrids sold as tissue-culture plantlets. The store's only Anthurium is warocqueanum.
- Why now: Soltech names velvet-leaf hybrids like 'King of Spades' as rising among collectors.
  ([Soltech](https://soltech.com/blogs/blog/the-ultimate-status-symbols-most-expensive-houseplants-in-2026))
- Price signal: Orange Lake Nursery lists an 'Ace of Spades' TC plantlet at $15.99 USD. A 'King of Spades' seedling
  sells for $124.99 USD and a finished plant for £69.99, so the spread from plantlet to grown plant is wide.
  ([Orange Lake, Ace of Spades TC](https://orangelakenursery.com/products/anthurium-ace-of-spades-plantlet-1),
  [Steve's Leaves](https://stevesleaves.com/products/anthurium-king-of-spades-x-doc-block-f2),
  [Cowells](https://www.cowellsgc.co.uk/product/anthurium-king-of-spades))
  The 'King of Spades' TC price did not show on the page (unverified).

  price per 100.

**2. Philodendron 'Caramel Marble' tissue culture.**
- What: a variegated Philodendron with caramel, cream and green marbling.
- Why now: it appears on several 2026 "must-have" lists and is widely stocked as TC by US sellers.
  ([Gardener's Yards](https://gardenersyards.com/rare-variegated-aroids-2026/))
- Price signal: US retail TC listings run from $17.99 to $143 USD. One wholesale listing on Faire shows $119 per
  plantlet against a $150 MSRP. The very wide range means some sellers are discounting hard.

- Difficulty: easy to medium, if your current lab already offers it.
- First step: check whether it is on your lab's next batch list. Price it below the US retail middle once
  converted to CAD with shipping. Canadian competitor prices were not checked today (unverified).

**3. "Winter humidity" cabinet kit.**
- What: a bundle of what collectors put inside a glass cabinet, such as an IKEA Milsbo. That means a grow light, a
  thermometer and hygrometer, a moss pole and aroid mix. Sell the inside, not the cabinet.
- Why now: Canadian homes drop to about 35% humidity in winter. One grower reports a stalled plant regrew at 60%
  inside a cabinet, and r/Aroids has an active thread of Milsbo builds running 70% to 90% humidity.
  ([House Plant Journal](https://www.houseplantjournal.com/?p=1398), [r/Aroids](https://lr.in.psf.lt/r/Aroids/top))
- Price signal: none found for a ready-made kit (unverified). Retailers such as Highland Moss sell aroid-mix and
  moss-pole bundles, which shows the pieces sell on their own.
  ([Highland Moss](https://highlandmoss.com/business-and-bulk-sales), [Plantscraper](https://plantscraper.com/collections/substrates))
- Difficulty: easy. No live plants are involved, so there is no shipping-death risk in winter.
- First step: price the parts from a Canadian wholesaler and see whether a kit lands under $100 CAD.
