Return & Refund Policy

At AgaPlantz, we take pride in providing healthy, carefully packaged plants and quality products. Because live plants are perishable and sensitive to environmental conditions, please review our policy carefully before placing an order.

Live Plant Disclaimer

All plants are living organisms and may naturally vary in size, shape, coloration, and variegation. Minor cosmetic imperfections, shipping stress, or slight leaf damage may occur during transit and are considered normal for live plants.

Product photos are representative examples and each plant is unique.

Choosing Your Shipping Method

Customers are responsible for selecting a shipping speed appropriate for their location.

Live-arrival coverage applies to transit of 5 business days or less, counted from the carrier’s first scan. Statutory holidays don’t count. If standard shipping to that address would normally exceed 5 business days, express is the customer’s responsibility and a DOA claim won’t hold.

We always pass on the lowest available shipping rate we can offer. Estimated transit times are shown at checkout — please review them, along with your local weather and the expected ship date, before completing your order.

Free shipping on orders over $180 applies to standard shipping only.** If express shipping is needed for your location, the cost of upgrading is the customer's responsibility, even if your order qualifies for free shipping.

Example: if your order ships on a Monday and standard delivery to your address is not expected to arrive until Friday, choosing express shipping is the customer's responsibility.

Shipping Delays

AgaPlantz is not responsible for delays caused by the shipping carrier, including mechanical issues, weather, service interruptions, or other circumstances outside our control.

Express shipping does not guarantee a specific delivery date. If your shipment is delayed by the carrier, shipping fees are non-refundable and cannot be adjusted.

We carefully package all plants to ensure they have the best chance of arriving safely, even in the event of a carrier delay.

AgaPlantz Acclimation Guarantee

Every plant we sell is covered for 30 days from the date it is delivered. If it doesn’t pull through in that time, we replace it.

- Plants under $200 CAD — free replacement. You cover replacement shipping, a flat $14.99.
- Plants $200 CAD and over — replacement at 50% of what you paid for the plant. You cover replacement shipping, a flat $14.99.

One replacement per plant, in the same variety wherever possible. If that variety has sold out, we issue store credit for what you paid for the plant instead.

To qualify we need clear photos of the plant, and we need you to have followed our Acclimation Guide — that is the one condition we hold to. Full terms are on the Acclimation Guarantee page, which governs how replacements are handled.

Returns

We accept return requests for eligible non-perishable items within 14 days of delivery.

For live plants, returns may be accepted only if the plant arrives significantly damaged, dead on arrival (DOA), or if the wrong item was received.

We do not accept returns or refunds for:

- Minor cosmetic imperfections or shipping stress
- Customer change of mind after delivery
- Plants that declined through clear neglect — left sitting in water, kept in direct sun, or grown without the humidity setup the Acclimation Guide asks for

A plant that declines after delivery for any other reason is not a refund — it is a replacement under the Acclimation Guarantee above, for 30 days from delivery.

Dead on Arrival (DOA) Claims

This covers plants that arrive dead or severely damaged. If that happens, contact us within 24 hours of delivery. A DOA claim is separate from, and often better for you than, the Acclimation Guarantee — it can end in a full refund with no shipping charge.

To submit a claim, please provide:

- Clear photos of the plant
- Photos of the packaging and shipping label
- Your order number

Claims submitted after 24 hours may not qualify as DOA, but your plant is still covered by the Acclimation Guarantee for 30 days from delivery.

Approved DOA claims may receive:

- A replacement plant
- Store credit
- Partial or full refund depending on the situation

Wrong Item Received

If you receive the wrong item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and your order number. We will work with you to provide a replacement, store credit, or refund where appropriate.

Lost or Missing Packages

If your package is lost in transit, please contact us and we will assist in opening an investigation with the shipping carrier.

Once a package is marked as delivered by the carrier, AgaPlantz is not responsible for theft, weather exposure, or damage occurring after delivery.
