Shipping Policy

At AgaPlantz, we carefully package every order to help ensure your plants arrive safely and in healthy condition. Because live plants are sensitive to shipping conditions, we ship orders carefully to reduce transit stress and avoid weekend delays.

Processing & Handling Time

Orders are processed and shipped between Monday and Wednesday only.

This schedule helps prevent plants from being held in shipping facilities over weekends, which can increase stress and transit damage.

Handling time is typically 1-3 business days depending on order volume and weather conditions.

Orders placed after Wednesday may ship the following week.

Delivery Time

Estimated transit time after shipment is typically 1-5 business days depending on your location and selected shipping method.

Estimated total delivery time:
- 2-8 business days from the date the order is placed

Delivery times are estimates only and may vary due to:
- weather conditions
- carrier delays
- holidays
- transportation disruptions

Shipping Locations

We currently ship within Canada only.

We do not ship to P.O. Boxes.

Shipping Rates

We offer the following shipping options within Canada:

- Standard Shipping - Flat Rate: $14.99 CAD
- Express Shipping - Flat Rate: $25 CAD

Orders over $180 CAD qualify for free standard shipping.

Shipping rates displayed at checkout are the final shipping charges for the order.

Replacement Shipping

Replacements sent under the 30-Day Plant Guarantee are charged a flat $14.99 CAD for shipping, whatever the plant cost. That is charged per replacement parcel, not per plant, so if more than one plant is being replaced at the same time it is still $14.99.

The 30-day guarantee window starts on the date the carrier marks your parcel delivered, which is the date shown on your tracking. Photograph your plant that day, as it comes out of the box. An arrival photo is required for a guarantee claim.

Shipping Carriers

Orders may be shipped using carriers such as:
- Canada Post
- UPS
- FedEx
- Purolator

Carrier selection may vary depending on destination and shipping availability.

Tracking Information

Once your order ships, tracking information will be sent to the email address provided at checkout.

Customers are responsible for monitoring tracking updates and ensuring packages are brought indoors promptly after delivery.

Live Plant Shipping Disclaimer

Live plants are natural living organisms and may experience temporary stress during shipping.

Minor cosmetic imperfections, bent leaves, soil movement, or temporary wilting may occur during transit and are considered normal shipping stress.

Customers should unpack plants immediately upon arrival and provide proper care and acclimation.

Weather Conditions

For the safety of the plants, AgaPlantz may temporarily delay shipments during periods of extreme heat, cold temperatures, storms, or unsafe shipping conditions.

By placing an order, customers acknowledge the natural risks associated with shipping live plants.

Lost or Delayed Packages

If your package is lost in transit, please contact us and we will assist in opening an investigation with the shipping carrier.

AgaPlantz is not responsible for delays caused by shipping carriers, weather conditions, or circumstances outside our control.

Once a package is marked as delivered by the carrier, AgaPlantz is not responsible for theft, weather exposure, or damage occurring after delivery.

Incorrect Shipping Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping information at checkout.

AgaPlantz is not responsible for orders delayed, lost, or returned due to incorrect or incomplete addresses provided by the customer.

Additional shipping charges may apply for returned or re-shipped orders.
