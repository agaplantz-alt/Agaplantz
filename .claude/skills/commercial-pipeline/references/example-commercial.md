# Worked example — a 15-second 9:16 pre-order Reel

A pre-order teaser for Alocasia Cuprea Red Secret Mint Variegated: hands open an AgaPlantz box on a windowsill shelf, lift out the sealed tissue-culture vessel, place it under a clear dome — and when the dome lifts, the acclimated plant stands in the same spot in warm sun. Three leaf macros, then the logo endcard. Angle: new arrival. Offer: pre-order. The plantlet gets its honest beat with an overlay before the swap.

Five references: hero plant on white, plantlet in its vessel, windowsill shelf location, hands sheet, logo file.

Read this for **density, tone and section behaviour** in a vertical social ad. The specific locks are written for this film; write your own from your own assets and failures. Structure carries over; the leaf map does not.

## What to notice

**The LEAF LOCK and the GEOMETRY LOCK are a third of the prompt.** That is proportionate. They are the sections that decide whether the film reads as one plant on one shelf or eight pictures of similar plants.

**The plantlet gets its own beat before the cover, and its own overlay.** That's the truth rule doing its job inside the creative rather than as a disclaimer at the end. The swap is still the hero moment; it just doesn't hide what ships.

**The leaf map is physical.** *Leaf one, left half, mint half-moon.* Not "beautiful mint variegation". This is what you rewrite when the batch comes back with the mint on the wrong leaf.

**Locks answer failures you should expect.** *No white, pink, yellow or cream* exists because "variegated" pulls the model toward albo. *Never touch a leaf* exists because hands grab foliage. *Dry leaves* exists because "fresh" produces rain. Write your own from your own batches.

**Everything critical appears twice.** The leaf count is in LEAF LOCK, in every segment, and in POSITIVE LOCKS. THE SHELF SPOT is in GEOMETRY LOCK, in every segment, and in the locks.

**The riskiest segments are S2 and S5** — the two where hands manipulate a clear object. Both have a cheap downgrade: S2 becomes a static vessel already on the shelf with hands withdrawing, S5 becomes the dome already gone and the sun arriving. The segment count stays eight. Know which shots are fragile before you batch.

**Judging the batch of four, in order:** is it the same five-leaf plant in S5–S8 with the mint where the lock says; is the plantlet honestly small in S1–S4; do the hands have five fingers and stay off the leaves; does the sun arrive exactly on the dome lift.
