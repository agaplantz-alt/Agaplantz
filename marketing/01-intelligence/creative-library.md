# Creative Testing Library

Every concept follows **Hook → Problem → Desire → Proof → Offer → CTA**.

**Status: none of these have run.** The Meta pixel has never fired, so there is no creative performance data for this brand. These are hypotheses ranked by expected fit, not proven winners. This file becomes valuable only once results are written back into it.

## Priority order for first tests
Test in this order — it front-loads the concepts that attack our measured problems (trust at checkout, TC niche ownership).

---

### C-01 — "The Unboxing That Proves It" · *risk removal*
- **Hook (0–3s):** Hands opening a box; the plant is visibly perfect. No talking.
- **Problem:** "Ordering a $300 plant online is terrifying."
- **Desire:** It arrives exactly as pictured.
- **Proof:** One continuous unedited shot, box → plant. No cuts (cuts imply hiding).
- **Offer:** Live-arrival guarantee.
- **CTA:** "Shop rare plants, shipped safe across Canada."
- **Why:** attacks the #1 diagnosed conversion blocker directly. Maps to the panel-consensus "UGC / unboxing" format.

### C-02 — "TC vs Acclimated, Explained" · *category education*
- **Hook:** Two plants side by side. "One of these costs $40. One costs $200. Same plant."
- **Problem:** Buyers don't know what tissue culture is or whether it's worth it.
- **Desire:** Get the rare plant for a fraction of the price, grow it yourself.
- **Proof:** Real timeline of the same cultivar TC → acclimated.
- **Offer:** Free Tissue Culture Starter Kit.
- **CTA:** "Start your first TC batch."
- **Why:** owns our highest-volume search term. **Watch:** this promotes the free kit — track downstream paid conversion (EXP-004), not kit claims.

### C-03 — "One Plant, One Photo" · *true scarcity*
- **Hook:** "Every plant we sell is photographed individually. Because no two variegations are the same."
- **Proof:** Rapid cuts of real inventory photos with SKU/date stamps.
- **Offer:** "When this one's gone, it's gone."
- **Why:** converts a genuine operational fact into structural scarcity — replaces the rolling-deadline discounting that trains waiting.

### C-04 — "The Canadian Problem" · *domestic sourcing*
- **Hook:** "Stop paying customs on plants that die at the border."
- **Problem:** Cross-border rare plant buying = phytosanitary paperwork, delays, DOA.
- **Proof:** Domestic shipping, 2–8 days, weather-held.
- **Why:** our customers append "canada" to searches constantly. Tier 1 evidence of the anxiety.

### C-05 — "Founder's Bench" · *trust in a young brand*
- **Hook:** Founder, unpolished, holding a plant: "People ask why our plants cost what they do."
- **Proof:** The actual grow space, the actual packing process.
- **Why:** counters "will this 4-month-old business exist when my pre-order ships?" Founder video is a panel-consensus format for young DTC brands.

### C-06 — "$14.99 Problem" · *offer test, not creative test*
- **Hook:** "Free shipping over $120."
- **Why:** tests whether shipping-cost shock is a real driver. Should be run as an **offer** test (threshold change) rather than a creative test — see EXP-003.

---

## Rules
1. **One variable at a time.** With ~24 monthly conversions we cannot read multivariate tests.
2. **Hook is the test.** Panel consensus: most performance variance sits in the first 3 seconds.
3. **Never test creative before the pixel fires.** Without conversion signal, cheap clicks look like winners.
4. **Kill on 3-second-hold-rate and cost-per-ATC early**, not purchases — we won't have purchase volume to judge quickly.
5. **Write results back here.** A library with no outcomes column is a mood board.
