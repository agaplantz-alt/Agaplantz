# Operating Rules

## Decision hierarchy (work top-down; never skip)
1. **Tracking accuracy** — 🔴 failing (3 defects)
2. **Profitability** — 🔴 unknown (no COGS)
3. **Offer** — 🟠 24.8% discount rate, no real guarantee
4. **Product-market fit** — 🟢 MER 5.79x, AOV rising, repeat rate tripled
5. **Traffic quality** — 🟢 non-brand, high intent, minimal waste
6. **Landing page / checkout** — 🔴 25.6% checkout completion
7. **Creative** — ⚪ nothing running on Meta
8. **Campaign structure** — 🟢 correct for scale
9. **Targeting** — 🟢 fine
10. **Optimisation details** — ⚪ premature

**Never optimise a campaign while a layer above it is red.**

## Evidence tiers (PART 17)
| Tier | Definition | Examples in this system |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Our own account data / documented case study | Shopify, Google Ads, GA4, Meta dataset reads |
| **2** | Experienced advertiser showing real account decisions | Practitioner teardowns with visible accounts |
| **3** | Detailed expert walkthrough | Store Growers structure guidance |
| **4** | Expert opinion / interview | Podcast claims, LinkedIn posts |
| **5** | General marketing content | Roundup blogs |
| **6** | Anonymous / SEO content | Uncredited "best practices" listicles |

**Tier 5–6 never outranks Tier 1–2.** Where our data contradicts an expert, our data wins — after checking our data isn't broken (which right now, it partly is).

## Strategy scoring (PART 18)
`Priority = (Evidence + Relevance + Impact) × 2 − Risk + Ease`, each /10, Risk inverted. See `01-intelligence/strategy-database.md`.

## Labelling discipline (PART 22)
Every claim is marked **Known fact** (measured, with source), **Inference** (derived, reasoning shown), or **Hypothesis** (untested).
Never invent performance figures, competitor spend, or expert results. "I don't have enough data to determine this" is a complete and acceptable answer.

## Change discipline (PART 15)
- One material change at a time per channel.
- Respect learning periods: Google 4–6 weeks, Meta 4–5 days minimum per ad.
- **At ~24 conversions/month, a week of data is noise.** Do not read daily.
- No budget change >20% in 7 days.
- Do not change things to look busy. A logged "no change, here's why" is a valid output.

## The scale rule
> **Any strategy that divides ~24 monthly conversions into more than one optimisation bucket is presumed harmful until proven otherwise.**

This is the most frequently-violated rule when applying expert content to this account.
