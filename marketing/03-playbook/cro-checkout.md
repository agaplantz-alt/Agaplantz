# CRO — the checkout leak

**The largest single opportunity in the business.** Bigger than any campaign change available at current spend.

## The number
```
August:  5,003 sessions
  ->  328 add to cart      6.6% of sessions
  ->  180 reach checkout   54.9% of carts     <- healthy
  ->   46 purchase         25.6% of checkouts <- roughly half the ~45-50% norm
```
**134 people/month abandon with payment details half-entered.**

Moving checkout completion 25.6% → 40% at current traffic ≈ **+26 orders/month × ~$212 paid AOV ≈ +$5,500/month, zero extra ad spend.**
For comparison, a 20% PMax budget increase (~$330/mo) at 3.2x reported ROAS returns ~$1,050 of *over-attributed* revenue. The checkout fix is worth roughly 5x more and costs nothing per month.

## Diagnosis, ranked

**1. No real live-arrival guarantee.** *(most likely)*
Homepage: *"Live Plant Guarantee — We're here to help if your plant arrives damaged."*
Shipping policy: *"Minor cosmetic imperfections, bent leaves, soil movement, or temporary wilting may occur during transit"*; no live-arrival guarantee; not responsible for address errors; re-ship fees may apply.
The homepage makes a soft promise; the policy withdraws it. A collector spending $300 on a living thing from a 4-month-old brand resolves that contradiction by leaving.

**2. Shipping-cost shock at the low end.** $14.99 standard, free over $120. Paid AOV ~$212 clears it — but Philodendrons start at $15, where $14.99 is a 100% surcharge. August shipping charges $540.72 / 63 orders = $8.58 avg, so many do pay.

**3. Delivery uncertainty.** "2–8 business days," Mon–Wed dispatch, four carriers, weather holds at AgaPlantz's discretion.

**4. Unverified — check first.** Express wallets (Shop Pay / Apple Pay / Google Pay) present at checkout? Pre-order items showing a clear ship date? Guest checkout available? **A manual walkthrough costs 20 minutes and may reverse this entire ranking.**

## Fix order
1. **Walk the checkout manually.** Desktop + mobile, cheap plant and expensive plant, pre-order and in-stock. Do this before changing anything.
2. **Write a real live-arrival guarantee** and place it *in* checkout, not just on the homepage. Bound it: 48-hour claim window, unboxing photo required, DOA replacement or store credit, weather holds already disclosed.
3. **Make the free-shipping threshold visible in cart** with progress ("Add $34 for free shipping"). Published data associates this with meaningful AOV lift as well as reduced abandonment.
4. **Show a delivery estimate before payment**, not a range in a policy page.
5. **Enable every express wallet.**

## Measurement
Primary: **checkout completion rate** (`sessions_that_completed_checkout / sessions_that_reached_checkout`), currently 25.6%.
Baseline the 28 days before any change. Read no sooner than 4 weeks — at ~180 checkouts/month, a week of data cannot distinguish a 5pp move from noise.
Secondary: AOV, refund/claim rate, support volume.
